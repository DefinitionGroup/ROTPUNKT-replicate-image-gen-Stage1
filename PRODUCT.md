# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Prospective kitchen buyers in Germany and English-speaking markets who want to see their future Rotpunkt kitchen before they commit, usually at home on a laptop or phone; secondarily Rotpunkt dealers and planners who show variants to customers in the studio. Users are consumers, not designers: they choose from curated options, they do not write prompts.

## Product Purpose

rotpunkt-visions.de turns a guided configuration (room, layout, sink and cooktop placement, front colour, handle, style, perspective, light, floor, accessories) into a photorealistic kitchen image in the Rotpunkt style, generated by FLUX with a Rotpunkt-trained LoRA. Users save images to a personal gallery, share them, upscale them and take them to a dealer. Success is an image the user recognises as "their" kitchen and as unmistakably Rotpunkt, delivered in well under a minute.

## Positioning

The only visualiser trained on Rotpunkt's real fronts, handles and materials (Frontfarben catalogue, FENIX colours, Buster & Punch and Tokyo handles) with a quality gate that verifies sink, faucet, cooktop and perspective before an image is shown. A generic image generator cannot truthfully claim the catalogue identity or the checked topology.

## Operating Context

Next.js 15 App Router, Sanity CMS for marketing content (content.md demo content behind DEV_CONTENT until the CMS is filled), Clerk auth, Supabase, MinIO, Replicate. Deploys via Jenkins from main to rotpunkt-visions.de. Locales de (default) and en, prefix always in the URL. The configurator is the reason the site exists; the marketing pages exist to lead into it and to carry Impressum and Datenschutz.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Wizard steps and their order are fixed: environment, room kind (with kitchen layout and sink/cooktop placement), colour, handle, atmosphere (style, perspective, time), floor, accessories, wishes.
- Generation is server-driven with a fixed seed baseline and a validator (see playbook-stabilisierung.md); the UI shows validating and quality-failure states.
- Clerk sign-in gates generation and the gallery.
- No light theme: the product is dark-only by decision (3 September 2026).
- Hero film is hotlinked from rotpunktkuechen.de until a local copy exists.

## Brand Commitments

Rotpunkt wordmark (white variant with the red dot), signature red #E30613 as the only filled action colour, black canvas, one italic serif word per headline, Manrope + Instrument Serif; the visual world is shared with rotpunkt Signature (RDDOT-3Dwebsite) and the Refero "Sequel" reference. German-first voice, English second.

## Evidence on Hand

Real generated kitchen images in MinIO and the Sanity ticker gallery (six per locale); Rotpunkt product photography in the Sanity "Über uns" cards; the Frontfarben and handle catalogues with training captions; quality-gate verdicts in Supabase. No customer testimonials, prices or dealer lists exist and none may be invented.

## Product Principles

- The configurator is the product; every marketing surface leads into /studio.
- One red action per view.
- Show Rotpunkt identity through real material, never through decoration.
- An image the gate rejected is never shown as a result.
- Content is data: marketing copy lives in one model, filled by content.md today and Sanity later.
